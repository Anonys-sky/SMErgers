A clear trail from chat to bill

Three checks. One shared version.

  1. Bring in the conversation. Upload a WhatsApp text export and match it to the job’s existing quote. Media is not needed.
  2. Review with the source in view. Check each suggested change against the highlighted messages, then price and route it for sign-off.
  3. Confirm, then reconcile. The client confirms or disputes from their page. Confirmed changes update the draft bill and quote history.
